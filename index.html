EverShelf è un inventario domestico: non fornisce diagnosi, non sostituisce il medico e non indica dosi.

⏱️ Cron watchdog (dead-man's switch)

                    
Optional. Every scheduled job pings this URL when it finishes, so a missing ping is what raises the alarm. Works with Healthchecks.io (hc-ping.com/<uuid>) and with a self-hosted Uptime Kuma push URL (…/api/push/<token>). The URL is a secret: it is not shown again after saving, and anyone who knows it can silence the alarm.
